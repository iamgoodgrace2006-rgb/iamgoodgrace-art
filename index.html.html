About

Who I am

I am an artist who uses creativity to express my thoughts, emotions, experiences and perspective on the world. My work reflects who I am and what inspires me.

What I make

Visual art that explores emotion, identity, beauty and everyday experiences. I experiment with styles, techniques, colours and materials to bring my ideas to life.

My influences

People, nature, culture, personal experience, music and other artists. I am inspired by how artists use colour, texture and storytelling to communicate.

What I want you to know

My work is more than something to look at. It is a form of communication. Each piece holds a feeling, an idea or a story, and I hope it makes you pause, connect and see the world differently.
